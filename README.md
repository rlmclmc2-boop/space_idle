# Orbital pressure arch — fixed visual evidence

Source: `feat/planet-single-side-ui@36abecd2`. Godot 4.7.2, 2048×768, OpenGL 4.5 Mesa llvmpipe. `planet-pressure-joint-1..4.png` are cumulative built states at phase 0; the `side25` and `side70` pairs show specified 25°/70° angles for station-only and full states. Scripts and logs are included. The ALSA warning reports absent host audio hardware; rendering used OpenGL.

The station's inner pressure cheeks, lower bearing gussets and recessed compression arch, plus workshop and shipyard load details, are in the source. The default-angle station aperture remains substantial. These static images do not show natural construction, wall-clock motion or a full orbit; they do not establish overall approval.
