# Sampling correction (2026-10-31)

The original 34 frames and MP4 are five post-action samples concatenated at an encoding frame rate of 8 fps. In `driver.gd.txt`, `click()` waits one process frame after mouse-down, sends mouse-up, then waits three more frames before `capture()` starts. Frames 0-5, 6-11, 12-19, 20-25, and 26-33 therefore show settled segments; they do not include the down/up transition or every rendered frame through the route change. The 4.25-second MP4 playback length is not measured wall-clock sampling duration. The original files and hashes remain unchanged. This evidence cannot rule out a transient blank frame, double layer, or focus anomaly at the moment of a route click. A separate same-process frame-by-frame capture is required for that claim. Screen capture perturbs timing and provides no performance baseline.
