# 敌方配对与阵型

- Paired enemy templates vary outgoing attack type only; `battle_design` pair metadata must agree with actual enemy weapons. `counter` still identifies the player's attack against enemy defence, not the enemy's outgoing type. Pairs preserve formation, defence, drops and level targets; added templates do not enter authored levels automatically.
- Enemy formation: legacy 10 slots retain the left-to-right rear row; new 15 slots use three rows of five in row-major order. Empty slots retain position; hull overlap is not resolved. Only final-group wipe clears level. Laser and hostile cannon projectiles fly fixed heading; player rail discharge settles its primary hit in the launch frame, with its screen-crossing afterglow visual only; missiles track their launch target in flight, then keep their last heading if it dies without retargeting; loss of lock does not stop their launch-to-cruise acceleration. Beams lock/charge/hit periodically; lost target/interruption resets. Normal wave preserves in-flight shots; final wipe clears them. Shield overflow hits HP in same attack.
