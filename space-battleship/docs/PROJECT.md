# PROJECT: stable gameplay contracts
NUMERIC SRC: authorized Excel sheets + user-approved rules.

SAVING
- Only timed and manual progress saves. Default interval is 1 positive integer minute, editable in Save settings. Use monotonic real elapsed time regardless of game speed, pause or focused state; late frames save once and schedule from now, never replay missed periods. Changing the interval resets the deadline without writing; manual saves leave the deadline unchanged. The interval persists with the next successful progress save.
- Business operations commit validated costs, rewards and state in memory without building/serializing a save or depending on disk success. Combat, settlement, pickup, upgrades, gems, navigation, startup, restart and exit do not save. Unsaved progress may be lost; settings show this warning and the last successful local save time.
- Timed/manual saves synchronously validate a flushed temporary file before safe replacement, preserve the previous file on failure and accept its backup on load. Failure reports explicitly without reverting business results or advancing the successful-save timestamp; retry only at the next deadline or a manual save. No asynchronous candidate is enabled.

PROGRESSION
- Loop: cruise -> encounter -> auto-combat -> resources/growth -> unlock. May hold/replay or jump to cleared/current level. Main retreats from stages 14–20 use at most one preceding wave spacing when adjacent attack/life scales grow by ≥2.5; other bands, travel and manual exploration retain configured retreat range. Each cruise/retreat segment and guard respawn interval is at most 3 X1 game-seconds; faster natural travel stays faster. Normal clear waits 3 game-seconds; immediate clear available.
- unlock.xlsx is sole threshold source: cleared=specified level cleared; reached=highest enterable level exceeds threshold; gems retain level-8 availability. Granted unlocks persist across threshold edits. Notices confirm individually after 3 monotonic UI seconds or manually; each next notice gets a new interval, independent of pause/speed/page. Tutorial archive uses earned unlock IDs and current table title/desc, grouped by system; previously earned entries survive reforge. Separate readUnlocks flags clear entry/category/entrance badges only when the player opens an entry, persist through saves/import/reforge, and migrate old earned entries as unread without rewards or notice replay.
- Level atkRatio/lifeRatio/resRatio interpolate by battle-point index: the first point uses the optional positive `entryAtkRatio`/`entryLifeRatio` for attack/life, otherwise inherits the previous level's final value (level 1 starts at 1); resource interpolation always inherits the previous endpoint, and the last point reaches the current level value.
- Optional monGroup `atkMultiplier`/`lifeMultiplier` multiply only that wave’s interpolated attack/life (including shields). Blank is 1; values must be positive and finite. Drops and the next level’s interpolation anchor stay table-authored. To connect main-stage bands, edit selected stage tail factors in Excel, preview `python tools/smooth_wave_curve.py`, then use `--apply` and the normal workbook import; the helper changes only selected wave factors.
- Manual hyperspace uses the selected exploration level for attack/life. Its ordinary monster iron/uranium and enhancement-fragment drops freeze the latest actually cleared mainline level at dispatch; highest reached does not count. Special drone/material rewards keep the exploration receipt level. Read existing authorized Excel ratios and drop probabilities; do not substitute new multipliers.

EQUIPMENT
- Equipment cards, current/next-level projections and details exclude game-time-settled buffs (same-target dwell growth and timed critical stacks); those changes do not invalidate the equipment UI. Combat retains them. Persistent/event-based modifiers and static attack/recovery rates keep their existing display rules.
- Module owns its actual equipment level; shared enhancement/history lives in profile. First defence slot is fixed armour with upgrades available. Other equipment swaps free, inherit module growth, identical items unlimited. Unequip: no refund/reset. Only enabled+equipped modules fight; ship controls enabled range. Swapping ship keeps combat and disabled tail modules. Preserve HP/shield remaining ratios on gear changes.
- Level-1 equipment row is base; target-level stats/cost use base × target multipliers, never iterate rounded previous level. x10 requires all 10 costs; MAX stops at affordable level. Entering cruise resets player weapon CD to full; countdown begins at encounter.
- Save journey level/node/cruise distance/hold destination, not enemy HP/projectiles/CD. Exception: a started paid manual hyperspace receipt persists its validated suspended main health, cooldowns, protection and debt; interrupted load refunds once and restores that completed main point without respawning killed actors. Unpaid requests are runtime-only and cancel on load/reforge. Old module level migrates only on load; old charge fields ignored. Save v4/enhancement schema v1 migrates old saves by retaining their fragment balance, clearing gem inventory/sockets/module history, and starting shared enhancement/history/branches fresh; other progress remains unchanged. New-schema load is idempotent.

COMBAT/ECONOMY
- Paired enemy templates vary outgoing attack type only; `battle_design` pair metadata must agree with actual enemy weapons. `counter` still identifies the player's attack against enemy defence, not the enemy's outgoing type. Pairs preserve formation, defence, drops and level targets; added templates do not enter authored levels automatically.
- Enemy formation: legacy 10 slots retain the left-to-right rear row; new 15 slots use three rows of five in row-major order. Empty slots retain position; hull overlap is not resolved. Only final-group wipe clears level. Laser and hostile cannon projectiles fly fixed heading; player rail discharge settles its primary hit in the launch frame, with its screen-crossing afterglow visual only; missiles track their launch target in flight, then keep their last heading if it dies without retargeting; loss of lock does not stop their launch-to-cruise acceleration. Beams lock/charge/hit periodically; lost target/interruption resets. Normal wave preserves in-flight shots; final wipe clears them. Shield overflow hits HP in same attack.
- Resource identity=ID. Drop multiplier and auto-pickup loss calculated in separate stages. Killed ship drops render/pick at explosion position; logical amount/position unchanged. Furnace drops separate: orange iron/purple gem core with timer ring; no battlefield name/count. Income rate=actual receipts in last 60 real seconds; spending excluded.
- Background app remains online at default multiplier. After exit, only full offline seconds grant chrono particles; awards are whole particles, with no partial particle credited at storage capacity. No other resource/research/exploration is granted offline. Each launch reports actual particles gained, including 0.
- Each furnace produces from its own non-furnace historical income peak (no self-feed); the input window, production and lifetime use game time, no offline catch-up. Resource receipts also retain a wall timestamp for the HUD; productionElapsed and production timestamps persist and pause with game time. Galaxy production reads the same game-time direct-income window. Chrono changes presentation speed while combat retains 1/60 game-second maximum steps. Scientist construction is visual only: no new resource or timer.
- Reactor level costs uranium and derives energy capacity; round each level's uranium fee upward before summing x10/MAX, so split and batch purchases share quotes, affordability and debit. Retain fractional stock. Integer allocations sum <= floor(capacity). Weapon/defense/smelting use own allocation. Only energy redistribution (manual, equalize, preset or automatic) preserves current HP/shield fractions, including while paused, without resetting hit/recovery timers. Zero pools stay zero; zero capacity discards any positive fraction rather than restoring it later. Redistribution loops must not accumulate healing. Upgrades, refits and repairs retain their existing health rules. Apply chrono multiplier once when real time becomes game time; charge particles by real time; insufficient balance leaves rest at default speed. Pause freezes game time.

ENHANCEMENT/CREW/UI
- `enhance_config.xlsx` is the sole enhancement numeric authority (generated into game_data). The first purchased level costs `cost_base` integer fragments; target purchase level L costs `cost_base × cost_growth^(L−1)`, with integer growth >= 1 (default 2). Shared purchased level applies to every eligible weapon/defense. The persisted level range ends at 9,007,199,254,740,991 because JSON double numbers cannot round-trip larger integers exactly; this is a serialization boundary, not a balance cap. MAX uses bounded binary search/geometric-sum arithmetic, retains unspent balance at that boundary, and never wraps. Each category freely orders its three unique effects; purchased + permanent planet bonus gates first/second/third at configured shared thresholds (default1/10/20), independently of equipment level. The system unlock gates every effect and branch; new/missing purchased levels default to zero, while existing purchase progress remains intact. Each branch node adds its configured base threshold (default10/20/30) to its independently authored position offset (default0/10/20), giving rank1 nodes10/20/30, rank2 nodes20/30/40 and rank3 nodes30/40/50. Reordering below a gate disables effects/branches but retains their kind-owned A/B choices; unopened effects remain reorderable. Strength uses that same effective shared level. Costs use only the next purchased target. Each effect has three independently saved A/B branches, freely switchable after its gate; reorder preserves effect identity. Same-stat A damage/capacity/Memory bonuses add, and probability additions are percentage points. The branch-specific coefficients, intervals, durations and chances also belong to enhance_config.
- Attack and damage events are distinct: a normal salvo counts once (table-authored missile count, one damage carrier per missile); each extra repeat salvo and each beam startup also counts once toward attack history. Beam ticks, secondary copies and chains only deal damage. Incoming history remains per incoming attack, including protected hits; deferred payments never count again. Histories survive ordinary refits/deaths/encounters; reforge resets enhancement/history/branches/fragments while retaining permanent planet compensation.
- One primary attack snapshots attacker damage/modifiers, critical outcome (including Critical30B's separate bonus roll), effect parameters and derived probabilities. All components/repeats/secondary copies inherit; only the primary consumes critical-next charges and arms critical buffs once. Repeat generations are preplanned at the source up to the configured bound, retaining probability and original-source multiplier semantics; execution never rerolls or recursively generates attacks. Repeat30B copies one full missile salvo or adds one secondary beam hit. A normal salvo and its secondary copies share one chain allowance; each independent repeat salvo has its own allowance while inheriting the root outcome. The first valid hit fans out to the configured targets from that hit position, even if it kills the primary. Visible carriers deal inherited damage only on arrival; dead destinations coast offscreen without retargeting or recursion.
- A continuous beam is one attack from startup until interruption. Bind its chain targets once at startup; every valid periodic hit propagates along those fixed visible links. A linked target’s death permanently breaks that link, without replacement; primary interruption ends all links. Charge alone deals no damage. Its damage, attacker bonuses, effect results, charge, growth parameters and damage interval are frozen; growth advances using those parameters. The configured 0.2s interval still receives cooldown modifiers (e.g. ×0.7 → 0.14s). Upgrades/new buffs affect the next beam; new charge is queued for the next attack. Each damage event reads current target resistance/reduction and runs hit handling, without generating new attack decisions.
- Proficiency10B compounds per full same-primary-target game-time interval, independently per weapon; extra/secondary/chain targets do not reset the primary lock. Critical20B stacks are capped and refresh as one timed group; Critical30B keeps its guaranteed event separate from the underlying chance that retains critical damage. Branch transient timers/targets are bounded by active modules and cleared by the existing full-health reset; dead/removed enemy source histories are pruned.
- Only eligible critical effects apply enhance_config base probability and effective-level critical damage; equipment-authored critical probability remains independent. Enemy stats remain table-authored. Proficiency/adaptation retain the authored log-counter growth and rounding rule. Automatic crew enhancement buys one affordable shared level per existing configured assignment cycle; direct drops, reactor condensation, galaxy/crew fragment modifiers and the existing furnace remain currency producers. Spending never enters production income.
- Adaptation10B records one global source-UID stack per incoming event, applies prior-stack additive reduction to that enemy's subsequent raw attacks, and caps the stack count. Adaptation20B generates each eligible module's short-lived neutral cover from its own real maximum plus its own Memory cap; generated covers never enter that base. Adaptation30B uses one shared resolver to raise existing matching body and granted Memory resistance, without creating resistance on neutral layers. Different shared DR buffs combine multiplicatively.
- Memory repair heals each eligible module from its own real maximum at configured game-time intervals; its overflow builds that module's neutral temporary protection up to its configured cap, without decay or recursive max/repair amplification. Without its explicit Memory20B branch, all enabled modules contribute to one resistance-free temporary pool. Incoming raw modifiers apply first, then neutral periodic covers, then Memory protection, then normal body resistance. Memory20B grants matching energy/physical resistance on the incoming event; mismatch removes it and prevents reacquisition for the configured lockout. Only qualifying contributor modules inherit the Adaptation30B boost. Absorbed protection damage is never deferred. Contribution ownership remains per module. Memory10B makes one shared roll per actual positive global restoration pulse; full capped pools do not generate free rolls. Memory30B counts enabled/equipped shield and armour modules, improves only overflow charge (not body heal base), and changes the Memory cap without recursive amplification. Losing eligibility, removing/disabling a module, or lowering capacity removes/clamps protection; it cannot repair another module.
- Delayed damage splits the full incurred post-mitigation damage by the configured integer-percentage curve. Ordinary shield capacity limits the actual immediate payment, so damage reaches HP only after current shields are exhausted; overflow from a capped shield module first uses remaining shield modules. Final armour still incurs overkill before clamping actual HP loss. Independent sources add installments into bounded global game-time buckets: the first installment occurs at least one configured interval later; quantization can extend completion by less than one interval. Each due tick pays its current portion, then makes one global configured chance roll to clear all remaining debt. Selected A-node probability contributions count once globally. Delayed30B overrides Delayed20B's ordinary no-clear rule; both its penalty and Delayed20B's passive reduction reference the underlying probability before forced clearing. Delayed10B's timed reduction starts only after an actual queue clear; no roll precedes the first due payment. Due damage runs before same-tick memory healing and death checks. Existing short cover and neutral temporary protection absorb already-mitigated debt first, then every installment uses current ordinary shields before HP regardless of its source bucket, without further mitigation or deferral. Recovered shields can absorb older HP-source debt; depleted/removed shields fall through to HP. HP zero remains fatal even if another defensive bar has a positive balance. Reorder/refit does not forgive debt; clear-state ticks continue payments. Protection/debt follow existing reset_player/reload/full-health restart policy, except the suspended main state owned by an active manual hyperspace receipt; pause freezes them and offline does not replay them.

- First planet unlocks on level 30 clear. Only idle crew explores; exploration/building crew cannot take another job. Exploration gives degree+XP, never a direct stat bonus. Pause freezes; offline does not catch up. `planet_build` owns applicability/order/construction/effects; threshold-crossing trips do not count toward construction. Buildings finish in a pending-activation state and release builders; effects require explicit activation. Existing built saves remain active. Pending activation persists across load and shows a planet navigation badge. Station activation defaults automatic normal trips on; the setting survives recall, crew replacement, reforge and load. Only the player toggle changes this preference after unlocking; an enabled setting without a crew does not dispatch anyone. Old saves without a preference default on only for a built station. Refinery affects direct iron/uranium drops only (production uses pre-refinery receipts); workshop uses the shared equipment stat layer. Shipyard reforge starts at planet.reforgeStartLevel (default 1), restores previously obtained unlocks through that stage via the existing unlock system, and retains crew/planet permanent progress. Earlier stages grant no skipped combat income. Seen unlock notices persist across reforge and load; only unseen unlocks prompt again. `planet_buff` derives permanent modifiers from that progress: conquest activates only after reforge; `luck:all` rows add permanent `hyperspace_luck` from each conquered planet (default +100), derived once from existing flags including old saves; equipment/hightech bonuses change effect levels, not actual levels or costs; free charge consumes no allocation and can exceed 100% (visuals stop at full); enhancement bonuses add effective levels without changing purchased levels or costs; they also count toward shared effect/branch gates. Buff descriptions come from ordered table rows; dynamic building formulas remain in `planet_build`.

- Enemy shields are transient independent layers. Authored `shield` capacity scales with stage lifeRatio; `shieldType` uses 0/1/2 resistance. Shared hit modifiers apply once; the shield consumes raw damage by its resistance and overflow receives body resistance. `shieldRecovery` restores a fraction of maximum per game second after `shieldDelay` since the last actual hit, including every beam period. Beam periods across mounts resolve in scheduled order before ordinary boundary impacts; synchronous secondary/chain hits share that period time. Target selection uses the defense layer at its query time; a whole beam still counts as one main attack. Pause/death freeze recovery, encounter respawn starts full, and absent fields mean zero shield. No fixed low-hit absorption is applied.

- Manual hyperspace requests charge only after revalidation at a non-guard main standby/travel/clear boundary: no live enemies, pending unlock notices or outstanding projectiles/repeats/delayed shots. Queueing/cancelling changes no balances; ownership/energy/route changes reject dispatch. Return freezes absolute main health, shield delay, cooldowns and module-owned transient effects, clamps to current lawful capacities, and preserves earned growth/income plus global RNG/serials. Reforge is unavailable during an active manual battle.

- Stage14 waves6–9 use Excel `authorAtkScale=0.125`; this author-only column shapes attack bands in the smoothing tool and is excluded from runtime projection. Tail multipliers include the shape; the following stage starts from the actual preceding tail. The life-ratio bands and production rules remain unchanged. Stage14 elite waves use one commander with eight mixed damage/armour escorts; the shorter boss hull has two weak escorts. Raw iron quotas remain equal to the preceding fleets; kill-time peaks and jewel receipts require calibration. Stage15 retains actor counts and reward quotas, shortens the single-hull opening, then uses one main hull with mixed escorts and a connected boss attack budget; the energy-shield boss still rewards weapon selection. Stage17 keeps its 20%/second shield recovery; a 0.6-second post-hit delay permits continuous 0.5-second energy hits to suppress recovery. Its elite/boss incoming weapon budgets and stage18 commander/guard budgets are authored in mon.xlsx. Stage15 life and stage17–20 tail attack/life factors are shortened and reconnected with the same smoothing tool. Stage19 is a release band with tapered per-hull life, two stronger lead hulls and weaker escorts. Stage20 retains the original fleet-to-single-hull structure with connected incoming attack budgets. Candidate pacing still needs complete fresh-save 1x acceptance; stage21+ and the stage20→21 transition remain outside this change.
