# PROJECT: stable gameplay contracts
NUMERIC SRC: authorized Excel sheets + user-approved rules.

SAVING
- Only timed and manual progress saves. Default interval is 1 positive integer minute, editable in Save settings. Use monotonic real elapsed time regardless of game speed, pause or focused state; late frames save once and schedule from now, never replay missed periods. Changing the interval resets the deadline without writing; manual saves leave the deadline unchanged. The interval persists with the next successful progress save.
- Business operations commit validated costs, rewards and state in memory without building/serializing a save or depending on disk success. Combat, settlement, pickup, upgrades, gems, navigation, startup, restart and exit do not save. Unsaved progress may be lost; settings show this warning and the last successful local save time.
- Timed/manual saves synchronously validate a flushed temporary file before safe replacement, preserve the previous file on failure and accept its backup on load. Failure reports explicitly without reverting business results or advancing the successful-save timestamp; retry only at the next deadline or a manual save. No asynchronous candidate is enabled.

PROGRESSION
- Loop: cruise -> encounter -> auto-combat -> resources/growth -> unlock. May hold/replay or jump to cleared/current level. Normal clear waits 3 game-seconds; immediate clear available.
- unlock.xlsx is sole threshold source: cleared=specified level cleared; reached=highest enterable level exceeds threshold; gems retain level-8 availability. Granted unlocks persist across threshold edits; notices acknowledged individually, no replay duplicates.
- Level atkRatio/lifeRatio/resRatio interpolate by battle-point index: the first point inherits the previous level's final value (level 1 starts at 1), and the last point reaches the current level value.

EQUIPMENT
- Module owns its actual equipment level; shared enhancement/history lives in profile. Equipment swaps free, inherits module growth, identical items unlimited. Unequip: no refund/reset. Only enabled+equipped modules fight; ship controls enabled range. Swapping ship keeps combat and disabled tail modules. Preserve HP/shield remaining ratios on gear changes.
- Level-1 equipment row is base; target-level stats/cost use base × target multipliers, never iterate rounded previous level. x10 requires all 10 costs; MAX stops at affordable level. Entering cruise resets player weapon CD to full; countdown begins at encounter.
- Save journey level/node/cruise distance/hold destination, not enemy HP/projectiles/CD. Old module level migrates only on load; old charge fields ignored. Save v4/enhancement schema v1 migrates old saves by retaining their fragment balance, clearing gem inventory/sockets/module history, and starting shared enhancement/history/branches fresh; other progress remains unchanged. New-schema load is idempotent.

COMBAT/ECONOMY
- Enemy 10 slots: config order left->right, one rear row; empty slots retain position; hull overlap not resolved. Only final-group wipe clears level. Laser/cannon projectiles fly fixed heading; missiles track their launch target in flight, then keep their last heading if it dies without retargeting; loss of lock does not stop their launch-to-cruise acceleration. Beams lock/charge/hit periodically; lost target/interruption resets. Normal wave preserves in-flight shots; final wipe clears them. Shield overflow hits HP in same attack.
- Resource identity=ID. Drop multiplier and auto-pickup loss calculated in separate stages. Killed ship drops render/pick at explosion position; logical amount/position unchanged. Furnace drops separate: orange iron/purple gem core with timer ring; no battlefield name/count. Income rate=actual receipts in last 60 real seconds; spending excluded.
- Background app remains online at default multiplier. After exit, only full offline seconds grant chrono particles; awards are whole particles, with no partial particle credited at storage capacity. No other resource/research/exploration is granted offline. Each launch reports actual particles gained, including 0.
- Each furnace produces from its own non-furnace historical income peak (no self-feed); production/lifetime use game time, no offline catch-up. Scientist construction is visual only: no new resource or timer.
- Reactor level costs uranium and derives energy capacity; integer allocations sum <= floor(capacity). Weapon/defense/smelting use own allocation. Apply chrono multiplier once when real time becomes game time; charge particles by real time; insufficient balance leaves rest at default speed. Pause freezes game time.

ENHANCEMENT/CREW/UI
- `enhance_config.xlsx` is the sole enhancement numeric authority (generated into game_data). The first purchased level costs `cost_base` integer fragments; target purchase level L costs `cost_base × cost_growth^(L−1)`, with integer growth >= 1 (default 2). Shared purchased level applies to every eligible weapon/defense. The persisted level range ends at 9,007,199,254,740,991 because JSON double numbers cannot round-trip larger integers exactly; this is a serialization boundary, not a balance cap. MAX uses bounded binary search/geometric-sum arithmetic, retains unspent balance at that boundary, and never wraps. Each category freely orders its three unique effects; actual equipment thresholds gate first/second/third, while effect strength and per-effect branch thresholds use purchased + permanent planet bonus. Costs use only the next purchased target. Each effect has three independently saved A/B branches, freely switchable after its gate; reorder preserves effect identity. Same-stat A damage/capacity/Memory bonuses add, and probability additions are percentage points. The branch-specific coefficients, intervals, durations and chances also belong to enhance_config.
- Attack and damage events are distinct: a normal salvo counts once (five missiles, five damage carriers); each extra repeat salvo and each beam startup also counts once toward attack history. Beam ticks, secondary copies and chains only deal damage. Incoming history remains per incoming attack, including protected hits; deferred payments never count again. Histories survive ordinary refits/deaths/encounters; reforge resets enhancement/history/branches/fragments while retaining permanent planet compensation.
- One primary attack snapshots attacker damage/modifiers, critical outcome (including Critical30B's separate bonus roll), effect parameters and derived probabilities. All components/repeats/secondary copies inherit; only the primary consumes critical-next charges and arms critical buffs once. Repeat generations are preplanned at the source up to the configured bound, retaining probability and original-source multiplier semantics; execution never rerolls or recursively generates attacks. Repeat30B copies one full missile salvo or adds one secondary beam hit. Each missile carries its own one-hop chain allowance inherited from the batch result; only a valid hit may select a new chain target, never an orphaned missile.
- A continuous beam is one attack from startup until interruption. Its damage, attacker bonuses, effect results, charge, growth parameters and damage interval are frozen; growth advances using those parameters. The configured 0.2s interval still receives cooldown modifiers (e.g. ×0.7 → 0.14s). Upgrades/new buffs affect the next beam; new charge is queued for the next attack. Each damage event reads current target resistance/reduction and runs hit handling, without generating new attack decisions.
- Proficiency10B compounds per full same-primary-target game-time interval, independently per weapon; extra/secondary/chain targets do not reset the primary lock. Critical20B stacks are capped and refresh as one timed group; Critical30B keeps its guaranteed event separate from the underlying chance that retains critical damage. Branch transient timers/targets are bounded by active modules and cleared by the existing full-health reset; dead/removed enemy source histories are pruned.
- Player base critical rate comes from enhance_config; only eligible critical effects add effective-level critical damage. Enemy stats remain table-authored. Proficiency/adaptation retain the authored log-counter growth and rounding rule. Automatic crew enhancement buys one affordable shared level per existing configured assignment cycle; direct drops, reactor condensation, galaxy/crew fragment modifiers and the existing furnace remain currency producers. Spending never enters production income.
- Adaptation10B records one global source-UID stack per incoming event, applies prior-stack additive reduction to that enemy's subsequent raw attacks, and caps the stack count. Adaptation20B generates each eligible module's short-lived neutral cover from its own real maximum plus its own Memory cap; generated covers never enter that base. Adaptation30B uses one shared resolver to raise existing matching body and granted Memory resistance, without creating resistance on neutral layers. Different shared DR buffs combine multiplicatively.
- Memory repair heals each eligible module from its own real maximum at configured game-time intervals; its overflow builds that module's neutral temporary protection up to its configured cap, without decay or recursive max/repair amplification. Without its explicit Memory20B branch, all enabled modules contribute to one resistance-free temporary pool. Incoming raw modifiers apply first, then neutral periodic covers, then Memory protection, then normal body resistance. Memory20B grants matching energy/physical resistance on the incoming event; mismatch removes it and prevents reacquisition for the configured lockout. Only qualifying contributor modules inherit the Adaptation30B boost. Absorbed protection damage is never deferred. Contribution ownership remains per module. Memory10B makes one shared roll per actual positive global restoration pulse; full capped pools do not generate free rolls. Memory30B counts enabled/equipped shield and armour modules, improves only overflow charge (not body heal base), and changes the Memory cap without recursive amplification. Losing eligibility, removing/disabling a module, or lowering capacity removes/clamps protection; it cannot repair another module.
- Delayed damage splits the full incurred post-mitigation damage by the configured integer-percentage curve. Ordinary shield capacity limits the actual immediate payment, so damage reaches HP only after current shields are exhausted; overflow from a capped shield module first uses remaining shield modules. Final armour still incurs overkill before clamping actual HP loss. Independent sources add installments into bounded global game-time buckets: the first installment occurs at least one configured interval later; quantization can extend completion by less than one interval. Each due tick pays its current portion, then makes one global configured chance roll to clear all remaining debt. Selected A-node probability contributions count once globally. Delayed30B overrides Delayed20B's ordinary no-clear rule; both its penalty and Delayed20B's passive reduction reference the underlying probability before forced clearing. Delayed10B's timed reduction starts only after an actual queue clear; no roll precedes the first due payment. Due damage runs before same-tick memory healing and death checks. Existing short cover and neutral temporary protection absorb already-mitigated debt first, then every installment uses current ordinary shields before HP regardless of its source bucket, without further mitigation or deferral. Recovered shields can absorb older HP-source debt; depleted/removed shields fall through to HP. HP zero remains fatal even if another defensive bar has a positive balance. Reorder/refit does not forgive debt; clear-state ticks continue payments. Protection/debt are transient and follow existing reset_player/reload/full-health restart policy; pause freezes them and offline does not replay them.

- First planet unlocks on level 30 clear. Only idle crew explores; exploration/building crew cannot take another job. Exploration gives degree+XP, never a direct stat bonus. Pause freezes; offline does not catch up. `planet_build` owns applicability/order/construction/effects; threshold-crossing trips do not count toward construction. Buildings finish in a pending-activation state and release builders; effects require explicit activation. Existing built saves remain active. Pending activation persists across load and shows a planet navigation badge. Station activation defaults automatic normal trips on; the setting survives recall, crew replacement, reforge and load. Only the player toggle changes this preference after unlocking; an enabled setting without a crew does not dispatch anyone. Old saves without a preference default on only for a built station. Refinery affects direct iron/uranium drops only (production uses pre-refinery receipts); workshop uses the shared equipment stat layer. Shipyard reforge starts at planet.reforgeStartLevel (default 1), restores previously obtained unlocks through that stage via the existing unlock system, and retains crew/planet permanent progress. Earlier stages grant no skipped combat income. Seen unlock notices persist across reforge and load; only unseen unlocks prompt again. `planet_buff` derives permanent modifiers from that progress: conquest activates only after reforge; equipment/hightech bonuses change effect levels, not actual levels or costs; free charge consumes no allocation and can exceed 100% (visuals stop at full); enhancement bonuses add effective levels without changing purchased levels, equipment eligibility or costs. Buff descriptions come from ordered table rows; dynamic building formulas remain in `planet_build`.
