# Budget correction after the 170/1000/0.25 probe

**Review-only recommendation, not an authored change or test result.** The [targeted probe](FOUR-WEAPON-RESULTS.md) found that 1000/3.5 cannon beat a same-type missile by only 0.30 s on a neutral 2 × 5000-HP heavy pair, while the beam won by over 8 s. A reactive target alone is too narrow a cannon role. Keep pulse **1.5 s**, missile **4 × 170 / 2.4 s** with its 0.4 s opener and same-target focus, and beam **35 per 0.25 s** with its 1 s first hit and additive 1x→3x ramp. The proposed correction is pulse **450 / 1.5 s** and cannon **1500 / 3.0 s**. The cannon's first hit and steady repeat remain a few seconds apart; no long charge, salvo rule change, or new mechanic is involved.

| Same-level level-1 sustained budget | Pulse, energy | Missile, physical | Cannon, physical | Beam plateau, energy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Prior probed candidate | 200/s | 283.3/s | 285.7/s | 420/s |
| **Revised candidate** | **300/s** | 283.3/s | **500/s** | 420/s |
| Matching type resistance (50%) | 150/s | 141.7/s | 250/s | 210/s |

On physical-resistant hull, pulse 300/s is 20% above resisted cannon 250/s and more than twice resisted missile 141.7/s. On energy-resistant hull, missile 283.3/s and cannon 500/s exceed resisted fully charged beam 210/s; the beam is weaker during the first 2 s of ticks. On a neutral long heavy target, cannon 500/s is 19% above fully charged beam 420/s and 76% above missile 283.3/s, before overkill. Using production `equipment_growth()` rounding at equal levels 1–100, the smallest pulse/resisted-cannon ratio is **1.143** and the smallest cannon/beam-plateau ratio is **1.111**. The corresponding level-1 ratios are 1.20 and 1.19. These are useful but not immunity to encounter geometry, mixed layers, buffs, or unequal equipment spending; the next short probe must check them.

The arithmetic explains why a much smaller pulse increase does not solve both aims with the beam left at 35/0.25/3x. Let `P` be pulse sustained damage and `C` cannon sustained damage; the beam plateau is `B=420/s`. For merely positive type preference and neutral long-heavy preference, require `P > C/2` and `C > B`, hence `P > 210/s`, or pulse damage above 315 at fixed 1.5 s. To leave roughly 20% headroom on **both** comparisons at level 1 requires `C ≳ 1.2B` and `P ≳ 1.2(C/2)`, hence `P ≳ 302.4/s` and unrounded pulse damage at least **453.6**. Under the current level-1 rounding, authored 455 projects to 460; 450 projects to 450. The recommended 450 deliberately lands near that coarse budget; rounding reduces margins at some later levels. It is a substantial **50% pulse damage increase**, so it must be reviewed as an opening pacing change rather than described as a tiny correction.

**Contrast only:** pulse **350/1.5** and cannon **1200/3.5** give `P=233.3/s`, `C=342.9/s`, and resisted cannon 171.4/s. Pulse would lead resisted cannon, but cannon remains below the unchanged beam's 420/s on a long neutral target. The 5000-HP pair would need at least five cannon hits per target instead of four under 1500 damage, making a broad ordinary-heavy cannon advantage unlikely. This contrast is an algebraic reason not to tune a single HP boundary around the earlier 1000-damage probe.

Existing 1x evidence offers a bounded timing estimate, **not a measurement** of this revision: the 1500/3.5 cannon cleared neutral 2 × 5000 HP in 28.13 s and the revised 0.25-s beam cleared it in 27.00 s. With the same eight cannon hits and a 3.0-s instead of 3.5-s cycle, an event-cadence estimate is about **24 s** for the cannon, leaving roughly 3 s over beam. One energy-resistant 5000-HP survivor would take four cannon hits, about 12 s before any scheduling/impact offset, versus measured 18.32 s for the unchanged 170 missile. These estimates require the agreed small old/native test; they do not justify writing Excel yet. The pulse buff may also compress early ordinary fights and must be checked alongside the cannon heavy result.

The original 170/1000/0.25 probe and its static old-layout cost warning remain recorded as historical evidence, not an approved production setup. No new Godot run or table edit was performed for this correction. The Windows window remains released until the parent chooses a budget for one short validation.
