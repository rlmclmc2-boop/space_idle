# Type-first budget correction after the focused probe

**Review-only priority candidate, not an authored change:** pulse **350 damage / 1.5 s**, missile **4 × 170 / 2.4 s** with the 0.4 s opening and same-target concentration, cannon **1200 / 3.5 s**, and beam **35 per 0.25 s** with its 1 s first hit and additive 1x→3x ramp. Pulse's 1.5 s baseline remains fixed. The previous 450/1500/3.0 arithmetic recommendation is withdrawn: it tried to make physical cannon beat energy beam on an **unresisted neutral** heavy target. Under the user's type-first design, ordinary heavy cannon should be compared primarily with the other **physical** weapon, missile. On energy-resistant hull, the beam is resisted and cannon is not. Cannon's spaced heavy hit and missile's distributed salvo are secondary differences; no very long cooldown, new resistance mechanic, or missile focus ban is proposed.

## Same-type and correct-defense budget

At level 1, `P=350/1.5=233.3/s` pulse, `M=4×170/2.4=283.3/s` missile, `C=1200/3.5=342.9/s` cannon, and fully charged `B=35×3/0.25=420/s` beam. The 50% matching-type resistance is applied per hit before damage ceiling; these continuous figures omit that ceiling and overkill.

| Target's exposed resistance | Energy pulse | Energy beam at plateau | Physical missile | Physical cannon | Supported long-run relation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Physical | 233.3/s | 420/s | 141.7/s | 171.4/s | Energy type has the advantage; pulse is **1.36×** resisted cannon. |
| Energy | 116.7/s | 210/s | 283.3/s | 342.9/s | Physical type has the advantage; cannon is **1.21×** same-type missile and **1.63×** resisted beam. |

Applying production `ShipDatabase.equipment_growth()` rounding at equal ordinary levels 1–100, the smallest pulse/resisted-cannon ratio is **1.292** and the smallest cannon/same-type-missile ratio **1.143**. The smallest missile/resisted-fully-charged-beam ratio is **1.212**. Thus the chosen budget preserves the direction through this finite level range without selecting one exact enemy HP boundary. It does **not** prove a clear-time ordering at every HP, group size, shield phase, slot loadout, enhancement, or target distribution. Pulse damage rises 16.7% from the present 300; cannon damage falls 20% from present 1500. Beam's 0.25 s tick is four hits/s: the first hit is at 1.00 s, each later tick adds **25% of base damage**, and the ninth hit at 3.00 s reaches 3x if lock persists. It is not compounding ×1.25.

## What the current samples do and do not confirm

The [focused 170/1000/0.25 result](FOUR-WEAPON-RESULTS.md) used **1000** cannon damage. Its neutral 2 × 5000-HP pair took ten cannon hits and cleared in 35.17 s, only 0.30 s ahead of the 170 missile, while beam cleared in 27.00 s. Raising cannon to 1200 **still requires five hits per 5000-HP target**, so that measured fixture cannot establish the ordinary-heavy advantage; its near tie is an open warning, not a pass. For a longer heavy encounter, the 1200 cannon has about 21% more sustained output than the same-type 170 missile, but the missile starts sooner and fires four travelling packets per salvo. Those mechanisms and per-target overkill can reverse a short result. A single energy-resistant 5000-HP survivor similarly still needs five cannon hits at 1200, so no improvement over the measured 1000-damage cannon's 17.58 s is established there. Do not author heavy enemy HP just above or below one cannon-hit threshold to force a result.

The original restored first-three-stage enemies already use physical-resistant hull in stage 1 and energy-resistant hull in stages 2–3, but these are not the unmounted 18-point draft. The [subsequent narrow old/native probe](PRIORITY-RESULTS.md) deliberately retained the 5000-HP near tie: cannon/missile clear in 35.17/35.47 s there, but 42.20/47.32 s at 6500 HP and 49.23/57.22 s at 8000 HP. Native rankings agree, and the 6500-HP relation holds at nominal `1e40`. This supports a readable ordinary-heavy cannon benefit on the two larger tested pairs while preserving the 5000 exception. It does not prove all HP values or arbitrary upgrades. The [18-point opening proposal](../../../space-battleship/docs/handoff/20261015-opening-18-priority-plan.md) uses these facts without mounting levels. No table edit or encounter integration occurred; the Windows window was released after the serial probe.

## Discarded contrast

Pulse **450/1.5** and cannon **1500/3.0** give 300/s and 500/s raw respectively. They were proposed solely to force cannon above a 420/s energy beam on neutral long targets while leaving pulse above its resisted output. That cross-type neutral victory is not a required role. The 50% pulse damage increase and faster cannon would widen global damage and early pacing much more than the type-first candidate above, so this contrast is **not recommended**. The four-weapon design must retain its layer-specific resistance and within-type mechanical hierarchy, not require every physical weapon to beat every energy weapon on neutral HP.
