## Actual divergence in the current asset

The last fixed current source is `wip/planet-thread-resume-20261101@fb3b6249e` (handoff now `528ae261d`); the same-source actual page is `archive/planet-resume-20261101@3e3b28e7:v22-armor-layering-fb3b6249/page-facilities-open.jpg`. Moving and bending those masses has not recovered the original broad continuous architectural band or its mass distribution.

## Draft decision for review

The draft restores the continuous wide belt as the principal building. Command is an inset observation alcove at upper left, heat an amber well at upper right, dock a radial open notch with a ship at lower left, and manufacturing a long open work channel in the lower-right belt. Wide armor masses alternate with narrower inhabited stretches; roof, deep support flank and cyan gallery remain continuous. The sphere stays detached and independently occludes the far arc. This is a visual proposal only. Independent construction ownership, runtime transitions, motion and all material implementation remain to be designed after the overall drawing is accepted.

The drawing was generated from the original complete image as a composition reference. It can introduce incidental details; the relations above, rather than incidental pixels, are the proposed structure.
